Through the pilot form: email, company name, invoice volume band and an optional note. No cookies and no analytics are set by this site.

Why and legal basis

To answer your pilot request (your consent, withdrawable at any time).

Invoice data you send us

If you take part in a pilot, you send invoice exports. We use them only to produce your audit, keep them no longer than 30 days after delivering your audit, and delete them on request. A data processing agreement is signed before any transfer. Please anonymise customer names where possible.

Retention and your rights

Pilot applications are kept for up to 12 months. You may access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing contact@cheldon.fr. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Processors

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data (such as IP address) to deliver it.
