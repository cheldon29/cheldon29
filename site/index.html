How it works

1. Send a CSV

2. We test it on your history

A walk-forward test on invoices the model has never seen, compared with a simple baseline.

Honest by design

Most tools promise "95% accuracy". We show the test instead. Forecasts are withheld for customers with too little history, and for your whole account if the model doesn't beat the baseline. Example of the reliability block in every audit (synthetic demo data):
